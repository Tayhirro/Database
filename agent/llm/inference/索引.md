# 术语索引（Inference）

导航：[README.md](README.md)

## A
- AWQ（Activation-aware Weight Quantization）：激活感知量化。见 [quantization/AWQ.md](quantization/AWQ.md)

## B
- BeamSearch（束搜索）：保留多个候选序列的解码。见 [decoding/BeamSearch.md](decoding/BeamSearch.md)

## C
- ContinuousBatching（连续批处理）：动态调度请求。见 [acceleration/ContinuousBatching.md](acceleration/ContinuousBatching.md)

## F
- FlashAttention：IO 感知的高效注意力实现。见 [acceleration/FlashAttention.md](acceleration/FlashAttention.md)

## G
- GPTQ：基于二阶信息的后训练量化。见 [quantization/GPTQ.md](quantization/GPTQ.md)

## I
- INT8：8-bit 整数量化。见 [quantization/INT8.md](quantization/INT8.md)

## K
- KVCache：缓存历史 Key/Value 以加速自回归。见 [acceleration/KVCache.md](acceleration/KVCache.md)

## P
- PagedAttention：分页管理 KV Cache。见 [acceleration/PagedAttention.md](acceleration/PagedAttention.md)

## Q
- Quantization（量化）：降低模型精度以减少内存和计算。见 [quantization/Quantization.md](quantization/Quantization.md)

## S
- Sampling（采样）：随机解码策略（Top-k、Top-p、温度）。见 [decoding/Sampling.md](decoding/Sampling.md)
- SpeculativeDecoding（推测解码）：用小模型草稿加速。见 [decoding/SpeculativeDecoding.md](decoding/SpeculativeDecoding.md)
