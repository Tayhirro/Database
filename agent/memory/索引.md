## C
- ConversationBuffer：对话缓冲区，存储完整对话历史。见 [short-term/ConversationBuffer.md](short-term/ConversationBuffer.md)

## E
- EpisodicMemory（事件记忆）：存储具体事件与对话。见 [long-term/EpisodicMemory.md](long-term/EpisodicMemory.md)
- EverMemOS：持久化记忆操作系统。见 [systems/EverMemOS.md](systems/EverMemOS.md)

## K
- KnowledgeGraph（知识图谱）：结构化知识存储。见 [external/KnowledgeGraph.md](external/KnowledgeGraph.md)

## L
- Letta：MemGPT 团队的记忆框架。见 [systems/Letta.md](systems/Letta.md)

## M
- MemGPT：虚拟上下文管理系统。见 [systems/MemGPT.md](systems/MemGPT.md)

## P
- ProceduralMemory（程序记忆）：存储技能与方法。见 [long-term/ProceduralMemory.md](long-term/ProceduralMemory.md)

## R
- RAG（Retrieval-Augmented Generation）：检索增强生成。见 [external/RAG.md](external/RAG.md)

## S
- SemanticMemory（语义记忆）：存储事实与知识。见 [long-term/SemanticMemory.md](long-term/SemanticMemory.md)
- SlidingWindow（滑动窗口）：保留最近 N 轮对话。见 [short-term/SlidingWindow.md](short-term/SlidingWindow.md)
- SummaryMemory（摘要记忆）：压缩历史为摘要。见 [short-term/SummaryMemory.md](short-term/SummaryMemory.md)

## V
- VectorStore（向量存储）：基于 embedding 的相似度检索。见 [external/VectorStore.md](external/VectorStore.md)
