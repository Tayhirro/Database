# 学术词汇 (Academic Vocabulary)

> 学术英语常用词汇，适用于论文写作、学术交流

## 2. 逻辑表达 (Logical Expression)

### 2.1 因果关系
| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 例句 |
|-----------|------|------|------|
| therefore | adv. | 因此 | Therefore, we must act now. |
| consequently | adv. | 因此，结果 | Consequently, the project failed. |
| hence | adv. | 因此 | Hence the need for reform. |
| due to | prep. | 由于 | Due to the rain, the match was cancelled. |
| owing to | prep. | 由于 | Owing to his help, I succeeded. |
| as a result | phrase | 结果 | As a result, we changed our plan. |
| lead to | v. | 导致 | Pollution leads to health problems. |
| result in | v. | 导致 | The accident resulted in injuries. |

### 2.2 对比关系
| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 例句 |
|-----------|------|------|------|
| however | adv. | 然而 | However, there are some problems. |
| nevertheless | adv. | 然而，不过 | Nevertheless, we continued. |
| whereas | conj. | 然而，鉴于 | I like tea, whereas he prefers coffee. |
| on the contrary | phrase | 相反 | On the contrary, it's quite easy. |
| in contrast | phrase | 相比之下 | In contrast, the second method is faster. |
| unlike | prep. | 不像 | Unlike others, she remained calm. |

### 2.3 递进关系
| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 例句 |
|-----------|------|------|------|
| furthermore | adv. | 此外 | Furthermore, the cost is too high. |
| moreover | adv. | 而且 | Moreover, it's not safe. |
| in addition | phrase | 另外 | In addition, we need more time. |
| besides | adv. | 此外 | Besides, I don't have money. |
| what's more | phrase | 更重要的是 | What's more, he's very talented. |

## 3. 学术写作短语

### 3.1 引出观点
| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| It is widely believed that... | 人们普遍认为 | It is widely believed that education is important. |
| It is generally accepted that... | 普遍认可 | It is generally accepted that smoking is harmful. |
| There is no doubt that... | 毫无疑问 | There is no doubt that he is talented. |
| It is worth noting that... | 值得注意的是 | It is worth noting that the sample size is small. |
| According to... | 根据 | According to the study, ... |

### 3.2 表达观点
| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| In my opinion/view... | 在我看来 | In my opinion, this is the best solution. |
| From my perspective... | 从我的角度来看 | From my perspective, this approach works. |
| It seems to me that... | 在我看来 | It seems to me that we need more data. |
| I would argue that... | 我认为 | I would argue that the evidence is insufficient. |

### 3.3 总结归纳
| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| In conclusion... | 总之 | In conclusion, the theory is valid. |
| To sum up... | 总结 | To sum up, there are three main points. |
| In summary... | 总结 | In summary, the results support our hypothesis. |
| All in all... | 总的来说 | All in all, it was a successful project. |
| Taking everything into account... | 综合考虑 | Taking everything into account, this is the best option. |
