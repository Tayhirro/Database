## 2. 会议与沟通 (Meeting & Communication)

### 2.1 会议相关
| 单词/短语 | 词性 | 释义 | 例句 |
|-----------|------|------|------|
| agenda | n. | 议程 | What's on today's agenda? |
| minutes | n. | 会议记录 | Please take the minutes. |
| brainstorm | v./n. | 头脑风暴 | Let's brainstorm some ideas. |
| chair | v. | 主持(会议) | She chaired the meeting. |
| adjourn | v. | 休会 | The meeting was adjourned. |
| postpone | v. | 推迟 | We need to postpone the meeting. |
| reschedule | v. | 重新安排 | Can we reschedule for Friday? |
| wrap up | v. | 结束，总结 | Let's wrap up the meeting. |

### 2.2 讨论用语
| 短语 | 释义 | 场景 |
|------|------|------|
| Let's get down to business. | 让我们开始正题吧。 | 开始讨论 |
| I'd like to raise a point. | 我想提出一点。 | 发表意见 |
| Could you elaborate on that? | 你能详细说明一下吗？ | 请求解释 |
| I'm afraid I disagree. | 恐怕我不同意。 | 表示反对 |
| Let me summarize... | 让我总结一下... | 总结发言 |
| Moving on to the next point... | 接下来我们讨论... | 切换话题 |

## 3. 商务谈判 (Business Negotiation)

### 3.1 谈判动词
| 单词 | 音标 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|
| negotiate | /nɪˈɡoʊʃieɪt/ | 谈判 | We need to negotiate the price. |
| propose | /prəˈpoʊz/ | 提议 | I propose a 10% discount. |
| compromise | /ˈkɒmprəmaɪz/ | 妥协 | Both sides need to compromise. |
| settle | /ˈsetl/ | 解决，达成 | We finally settled the deal. |
| reject | /rɪˈdʒekt/ | 拒绝 | They rejected our offer. |
| accept | /əkˈsept/ | 接受 | We accept your terms. |
| offer | /ˈɒfər/ | 提供，出价 | What can you offer? |
| counter | /ˈkaʊntər/ | 反驳，还价 | They countered with a lower price. |

### 3.2 谈判用语
| 短语 | 释义 | 场景 |
|------|------|------|
| We're prepared to offer... | 我们准备提供... | 出价 |
| Is there any room for negotiation? | 还有商量的余地吗？ | 讨价还价 |
| That's not acceptable to us. | 这对我们来说不可接受。 | 拒绝 |
| Can we meet halfway? | 我们能各让一步吗？ | 妥协 |
| We have a deal. | 成交。 | 达成协议 |
| Let's put it in writing. | 让我们书面确认。 | 签约 |

## 5. 商务邮件 (Business Email)

### 5.1 邮件开头
| 短语 | 释义 | 适用场景 |
|------|------|---------|
| I am writing to inquire about... | 我写信是想咨询... | 询问 |
| Thank you for your email dated... | 感谢您...日期的邮件 | 回复 |
| With reference to... | 关于... | 引用话题 |
| Further to our conversation... | 关于我们的谈话... | 跟进 |
| I am pleased to inform you... | 我很高兴通知您... | 好消息 |
| I regret to inform you... | 我很遗憾通知您... | 坏消息 |

### 5.2 邮件结尾
| 短语 | 释义 | 适用场景 |
|------|------|---------|
| I look forward to hearing from you. | 期待您的回复。 | 通用 |
| Please do not hesitate to contact me. | 如有问题请随时联系。 | 表示友好 |
| Thank you for your time and consideration. | 感谢您的时间和考虑。 | 正式感谢 |
| I would appreciate your prompt reply. | 感谢您的及时回复。 | 催促 |
| Best regards / Kind regards | 此致敬意 | 结束语 |
