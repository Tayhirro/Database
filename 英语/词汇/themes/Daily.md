# 日常词汇 (Daily Vocabulary)

| 单词 | 音标    | 词性 | 释义   | 例句                          |
| ---- | ------- | ---- | ------ | ----------------------------- |
| wise | /waɪz/  | adj. | 明智的；聪明的 | It's wise to save money for emergencies. |
| entente | /ɒnˈtɑːnt/ | n. | 协定；协议 | The two countries signed a mutual defense entente. |
