# P 开头的单词

| 单词       | 音标        | 词性 | 释义     | 例句                                           |
| ---------- | ----------- | ---- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| posit | /ˈpɒzɪt/ | v. | 假定；设想；提出 | The researchers posit that early intervention improves long-term outcomes. |
| plethora | /ˈpleθərə/ | n. | 过多；过剩；大量 | The report identified a plethora of problems in the healthcare system. |
| proactive | /ˌprəʊˈæktɪv/ | adj. | 主动的；积极的；先发制人的 | Successful employees are always proactive in solving problems. |
| percentile | /pəˈsentaɪl/ | n.  | 百分位数；百分等级 | The student scored in the 95th percentile on the standardized test. |
| Perplexity | /pəˈpleksəti/ | n.  | 困惑度；混乱度 | Lower perplexity indicates better language model performance. |
| propel     | /prəˈpel/    | v.  | 推动；推进 | The new findings will propel further research in this area. |
