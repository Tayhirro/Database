# I 开头的单词

| 单词   | 音标      | 词性 | 释义     | 例句                                           |
| ------ | --------- | ---- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| intuitive | /ɪnˈtjuːɪtɪv/ | adj. | 直观的；直觉的；易于理解的 | The software's intuitive interface makes it easy for beginners to use. |
| induce | /ɪnˈdjuːs/ | v. | 引起；导致；归纳 | The treatment may induce side effects in some patients. |
| inertial | /ɪˈnɜːʃl/ | adj. | 惯性的；惰性的 | The inertial navigation system provides accurate positioning without external signals. |
| invariance | /ɪnˈveəriəns/ | n. | 不变性；恒定性 | The model learns representations with translation invariance. |
| intervention | /ˌɪntəˈvenʃn/ | n. | 干预；介入；调停 | Early intervention programs can significantly improve student outcomes. |
| imbue | /ɪmˈbjuː/ | v. | 灌输；使充满；浸染 | The teacher tried to imbue students with a love of learning. |
| in conjunction with | /ɪn kənˈdʒʌŋkʃn wɪð/ | phr. | 与…一起；共同；联合 | The library can be used in conjunction with other tools for better results. |
| impede | /ɪmˈpiːd/ | v.   | 阻碍；妨碍 | Limited computational resources may impede the training process. |
| improvised | /ˈɪmprəvaɪzd/ | v. (improvise 过去式/分词) / adj. | 即兴创作（音乐、台词等）；临时凑合、权宜制作 | The team improvised a makeshift shelter from whatever materials were at hand. |
| intractably | /ɪnˈtræktəbli/ | adv. | 棘手地；难以处理地；倔强地（intractable 的副词，指问题"硬到没法解决或控制"） | Some optimization problems in compiler design are intractably hard, so we settle for approximate heuristics. |
| infeasible | /ɪnˈfiːzəbl/ | adj. | 不可行的；行不通的（feasible 的否定，指方案"做不成"而非"难做"） | A brute-force search over all register assignments is infeasible for functions with more than a few variables. |
