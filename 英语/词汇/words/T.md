# T 开头的单词

| 单词   | 音标     | 词性 | 释义     | 例句                                           |
| ------ | -------- | ---- | -------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| tangential | /tænˈdʒenʃl/ | adj. | 切线的；离题的；间接相关的 | The discussion went off on several tangential topics before returning to the main issue. |
| to which extent | /tuː wɪtʃ ɪkˈstent/ | phr. | 在多大程度上；到什么程度 | The study aims to determine to which extent social media affects teenagers' mental health. |
| through the lens of | /θruː ðə lenz ɒv/ | phr. | 通过…的视角；从…的角度 | We can analyze social problems through the lens of sociology. |
