# A 开头的单词

| 单词   | 音标     | 词性 | 释义       | 例句                                           |
| ------ | -------- | ---- | ---------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| accountability | /əˌkaʊntəˈbɪləti/ | n. | 责任；问责制；可说明性 | The new policy emphasizes accountability in corporate governance. |
| agnostic | /æɡˈnɒstɪk/ | adj. | 不可知论的；与特定系统无关的 | The framework is designed to be agnostic to the underlying hardware. |
| alleviate | /əˈliːvieɪt/ | v. | 减轻；缓解；缓和 | The new policy aims to alleviate poverty in rural areas. |
| amortize | /ˈæmətaɪz/ | v. | 摊销；分期偿还；减弱 | The company decided to amortize the cost of the equipment over five years. |
| appeal | /əˈpiːl/ | v./n. | 呼吁；恳求；上诉；吸引力 | The government appealed to the public to stay calm. / The idea of working abroad holds little appeal for me. |
| audit | /ˈɔːdɪt/ | n./v. | 审计；查账；审查 | The team will audit the financial records to ensure compliance with regulations. |
| allude | /əˈluːd/ | v.   | 暗示；间接提到 | The author alludes to previous research without direct citation. |
| analogous | /əˈnæləɡəs/ | adj. | 类似的；相似的 | The neural network structure is analogous to the human brain. |
| ameliorate | /əˈmiːliəreɪt/ | v. | 改善；改进 | The new technique ameliorates the model's performance significantly. |
| auxiliary | /ɔːɡˈzɪljəri/ | adj. | 辅助的；附加的 | The model uses auxiliary information to improve predictions. |
| academia | /ˌækəˈdiːmiə/ | n. | 学术界；学术圈；学术环境 | She left academia after a decade of research to join the industry. |
| advent | /ˈædvent/ | n. | （重要事物、时代的）到来；出现；降临 | The advent of deep learning transformed natural language processing almost overnight. |
