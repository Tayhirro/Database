# 常见语法错误 (Common Errors)

> 本页收集中国学习者常犯的语法错误，帮助规避"中式英语"

## 1. 时态错误

### 1.1 现在完成时 vs 一般过去时

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| ~~I have seen him yesterday.~~ | I saw him yesterday. | 有明确过去时间用一般过去时 |
| ~~I went to Paris three times.~~ | I have been to Paris three times. | 强调经历对现在的影响用完成时 |
| ~~I am here since 2020.~~ | I have been here since 2020. | since 搭配完成时 |

### 1.2 状态动词不用进行时

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| ~~I am knowing him.~~ | I know him. | know 是状态动词 |
| ~~I am loving it.~~ | I love it. | love 是状态动词 |
| ~~I am having a car.~~ | I have a car. | have 表"拥有"时不用进行时 |

**常见状态动词**：know, love, like, hate, want, need, believe, understand, remember, belong, own, have(拥有)

---

## 2. 主谓一致错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| ~~The news are good.~~ | The news is good. | news 是不可数名词 |
| ~~Everyone have arrived.~~ | Everyone has arrived. | everyone 用单数 |
| ~~The police is coming.~~ | The police are coming. | police 是集体名词，用复数 |
| ~~Mathematics are difficult.~~ | Mathematics is difficult. | 学科名词用单数 |
| ~~One of the students have left.~~ | One of the students has left. | one of 主语是 one |
| ~~There is many people.~~ | There are many people. | people 是复数 |

---

## 3. 冠词错误

### 3.1 漏用冠词

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| ~~I am student.~~ | I am a student. | 可数名词单数需要冠词 |
| ~~He plays piano.~~ | He plays the piano. | 乐器前用 the |
| ~~Sun rises in east.~~ | The sun rises in the east. | 独一无二的事物用 the |

### 3.2 多用冠词

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| ~~I like the music.~~ | I like music. (泛指) | 泛指概念不用冠词 |
| ~~I had the lunch.~~ | I had lunch. | 三餐前一般不用冠词 |
| ~~I went to the school.~~ | I went to school. | 表"上学"不用冠词 |

---

## 4. 介词错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| ~~I arrived to Beijing.~~ | I arrived in Beijing. | arrive in/at |
| ~~I'm interested on it.~~ | I'm interested in it. | interested in |
| ~~different with~~ | different from | different from |
| ~~listen the music~~ | listen to the music | listen to |
| ~~discuss about~~ | discuss | discuss 直接接宾语 |
| ~~enter into the room~~ | enter the room | enter 直接接宾语 |
| ~~marry with sb~~ | marry sb | marry 直接接宾语 |

---

## 5. 代词错误

### 5.1 反身代词

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| ~~I and my friend went.~~ | My friend and I went. | I 放后面 |
| ~~He hurt him.~~ | He hurt himself. | 主语宾语同一人用反身代词 |

### 5.2 it 的用法

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| ~~Is difficult to learn.~~ | It is difficult to learn. | 形式主语 it |
| ~~I found very easy to do.~~ | I found it very easy to do. | 形式宾语 it |

---

## 6. 动词形式错误

### 6.1 动词不定式 vs 动名词

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| ~~enjoy to do~~ | enjoy doing | enjoy 后接动名词 |
| ~~want doing~~ | want to do | want 后接不定式 |
| ~~suggest to do~~ | suggest doing | suggest 后接动名词 |
| ~~decide doing~~ | decide to do | decide 后接不定式 |

**接动名词的动词**：enjoy, finish, avoid, mind, practice, suggest, consider, keep
**接不定式的动词**：want, hope, wish, decide, agree, plan, promise, refuse, learn

### 6.2 使役动词

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| ~~let me to go~~ | let me go | let 后接动词原形 |
| ~~make him to do~~ | make him do | make 后接动词原形 |
| ~~have him to do~~ | have him do | have 后接动词原形 |

---

## 7. 句子结构错误

### 7.1 缺少主语

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| ~~Is very important.~~ | It is very important. | 英语句子必须有主语 |
| ~~Raining now.~~ | It is raining now. | 天气用 it 作主语 |

### 7.2 双重否定

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| ~~I don't know nothing.~~ | I don't know anything. | 避免双重否定 |
| ~~I can't see nobody.~~ | I can't see anybody. | 避免双重否定 |

### 7.3 状语位置

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| ~~I very much like it.~~ | I like it very much. | 副词放动词/宾语后 |
| ~~I have finished already.~~ | I have already finished. | already 放在 have 和动词之间 |

---

## 8. 从句错误

### 8.1 定语从句

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| ~~The man which came...~~ | The man who came... | 先行词是人用 who |
| ~~The book who I bought...~~ | The book which/that I bought... | 先行词是物用 which/that |
| ~~The man I met him...~~ | The man I met... | 关系代词已代替 him |

### 8.2 名词性从句

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| ~~I don't know where is he.~~ | I don't know where he is. | 从句用陈述语序 |
| ~~I wonder that he will come.~~ | I wonder whether he will come. | 疑问用 whether，不用 that |

---

## 9. 中式英语典型错误

| 错误 | 正确 | 中文思维 |
|------|------|---------|
| ~~open the light~~ | turn on the light | "开灯" |
| ~~close the light~~ | turn off the light | "关灯" |
| ~~long time no see~~ | (口语可接受) It's been a long time. | "好久不见" |
| ~~give you some color see see~~ | teach you a lesson | "给你点颜色看看" |
| ~~I very like it~~ | I like it very much | "我很喜欢" |
| ~~My English is poor~~ | My English is not very good | "我英语不好" |
| ~~You can eat your medicine~~ | You can take your medicine | "吃药" |

---

## 学习建议

1. **对照检查**：写作后对照本页检查常见错误
2. **积累纠错**：建立个人错误笔记本
3. **多读原文**：通过阅读培养语感
4. **主动练习**：刻意练习容易出错的结构
