# 从句类型 (Clause Types)

> 从句 = 在复合句中充当某一成分的句子

## 从句分类概览

```
从句 (Clause)
├─ 名词性从句 (Noun Clause)
│   ├─ 主语从句 (Subject Clause)
│   ├─ 宾语从句 (Object Clause)
│   ├─ 表语从句 (Predicative Clause)
│   └─ 同位语从句 (Appositive Clause)
├─ 定语从句 (Attributive Clause)
│   ├─ 限制性定语从句
│   └─ 非限制性定语从句
└─ 状语从句 (Adverbial Clause)
    ├─ 时间状语从句
    ├─ 原因状语从句
    ├─ 条件状语从句
    ├─ 让步状语从句
    ├─ 目的状语从句
    └─ 结果状语从句
```

---

## 1. 定语从句 (Attributive Clause)

### 1.1 基本概念
定语从句修饰名词或代词，由关系词引导。

**关系代词**：who, whom, whose, which, that
**关系副词**：when, where, why

### 1.2 关系词选择

| 先行词 | 关系词 | 例句 |
|--------|--------|------|
| 人(主语) | who/that | The man **who** is talking is my father. |
| 人(宾语) | whom/who/that | The girl **(whom)** I met is kind. |
| 人(所有格) | whose | The boy **whose** book was lost is sad. |
| 物(主语/宾语) | which/that | The book **which/that** I bought is useful. |
| 物(所有格) | whose/of which | The house **whose** roof is red... |
| 时间 | when | I remember the day **when** we met. |
| 地点 | where | This is the place **where** I grew up. |
| 原因 | why | The reason **why** he left is unknown. |

### 1.3 只能用 that 的情况
- 先行词是 all, everything, nothing, something, anything 等
- 先行词有最高级修饰
- 先行词有序数词修饰
- 先行词同时有人和物

### 1.4 限制性 vs 非限制性

| 类型 | 特点 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 限制性 | 无逗号，必须有 | The girl who is singing is my sister. |
| 非限制性 | 有逗号，可省略 | My sister, who is a doctor, lives in Beijing. |

## 2. 名词性从句 (Noun Clause)

### 2.1 主语从句 (Subject Clause)
**位置**：从句作主语

**引导词**：that, whether, what, who, which, when, where, why, how

**例句**：
- **That he will come** is certain.
- **What he said** surprised me.
- **Whether he will come** is unknown.

**形式主语 it**：
- It is certain **that he will come**.
- It surprised me **what he said**.

### 2.2 宾语从句 (Object Clause)
**位置**：从句作宾语

**引导词**：that(可省略), whether/if, what, who, which, when, where, why, how

**例句**：
- I think **(that) he is right**.
- I don't know **if/whether he will come**.
- Tell me **what you want**.

**注意**：
- 介词后只用 whether，不用 if
- 否定前移：I don't think he is right. (不说 I think he isn't right.)

### 2.3 表语从句 (Predicative Clause)
**位置**：从句作表语（在系动词后）

**例句**：
- The fact is **that we need more time**.
- The question is **whether we can finish it**.
- That's **what I want to say**.

### 2.4 同位语从句 (Appositive Clause)
**位置**：解释说明抽象名词（news, fact, idea, hope, belief 等）

**引导词**：that（最常用）, whether, what 等

**例句**：
- The news **that he won the prize** spread quickly.
- I have no idea **when he will come**.

**与定语从句区别**：
- 同位语从句：that 不作成分，可换成"即"
- 定语从句：that 作成分（主/宾）

## 3. 状语从句 (Adverbial Clause)

### 3.1 时间状语从句
**引导词**：when, while, as, before, after, until, since, as soon as

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|--------|------|------|
| when | 当...时 | When I arrived, he left. |
| while | 在...期间 | While I was reading, he was sleeping. |
| as | 一边...一边 | As he walked, he sang. |
| before | 在...之前 | Finish it before you leave. |
| after | 在...之后 | I went home after I finished. |
| until | 直到 | Wait until he comes. |
| since | 自从 | I have lived here since 2010. |
| as soon as | 一...就 | Call me as soon as you arrive. |

### 3.2 原因状语从句
**引导词**：because, since, as, for

| 引导词 | 语气 | 位置 |
|--------|------|------|
| because | 最强，直接原因 | 可前可后 |
| since | 既然（已知原因） | 常在句首 |
| as | 由于（顺便提及） | 常在句首 |
| for | 推断原因 | 只能在后 |

### 3.3 条件状语从句
**引导词**：if, unless, as long as, in case, provided that

**真实条件句**：
- If it rains, we will stay home.

**虚拟条件句**：见 [Mood.md](Mood.md)

### 3.4 让步状语从句
**引导词**：although, though, even though, even if, while, no matter...

**例句**：
- **Although** he is young, he is capable.
- **No matter what** you say, I won't change my mind.
- Young **as** he is, he is capable. (倒装)

### 3.5 目的状语从句
**引导词**：so that, in order that, for fear that, lest

**例句**：
- I got up early **so that** I could catch the bus.
- He worked hard **in order that** he might succeed.

### 3.6 结果状语从句
**引导词**：so...that, such...that

**结构**：
- so + 形容词/副词 + that
- such + (a/an) + 名词 + that

**例句**：
- He is **so** clever **that** everyone likes him.
- It was **such** a good book **that** I read it twice.

## 从句识别流程

```
步骤1: 找引导词
步骤2: 判断从句功能
├─ 修饰名词 → 定语从句
├─ 修饰动词/句子(表时间/原因/条件等) → 状语从句
└─ 充当名词成分(主/宾/表/同位语) → 名词性从句
```
