# 固定搭配 (Collocations)

> 固定搭配是指经常一起出现的词语组合，掌握搭配能让表达更地道

## 1. 动词 + 介词搭配

### 常用动词介词搭配

| 动词 | 介词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|
| agree | with | 同意(某人) | I agree with you. |
| agree | on | 就...达成一致 | We agreed on the plan. |
| agree | to | 同意(提议) | He agreed to my request. |
| apologize | for | 为...道歉 | I apologize for being late. |
| apply | for | 申请 | She applied for the job. |
| believe | in | 相信(存在/价值) | I believe in you. |
| belong | to | 属于 | This book belongs to me. |
| care | about | 关心 | I care about your health. |
| care | for | 照顾/喜欢 | She cares for her parents. |
| consist | of | 由...组成 | Water consists of H2O. |
| depend | on | 依赖/取决于 | It depends on the weather. |
| differ | from | 与...不同 | This differs from that. |
| dream | of/about | 梦想 | I dream of becoming a doctor. |
| insist | on | 坚持 | He insisted on paying. |
| listen | to | 听 | Listen to me carefully. |
| look | at | 看 | Look at the picture. |
| look | for | 寻找 | I'm looking for my keys. |
| look | after | 照顾 | She looks after her baby. |
| rely | on | 依靠 | You can rely on me. |
| succeed | in | 在...成功 | He succeeded in passing. |
| wait | for | 等待 | I'm waiting for you. |

### 易混淆搭配

| 搭配 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| remind sb of sth | 使某人想起 | This reminds me of my childhood. |
| remind sb to do | 提醒某人做 | Remind me to call him. |
| provide sb with sth | 给某人提供 | They provided us with food. |
| provide sth for sb | 为某人提供 | They provided food for us. |
| prevent sb from doing | 阻止某人做 | Rain prevented us from going out. |
| stop sb from doing | 阻止某人做 | Nothing can stop me from trying. |

## 4. 常用固定短语

### make 短语
| 短语 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| make a decision | 做决定 | Make a decision now. |
| make a mistake | 犯错误 | Everyone makes mistakes. |
| make progress | 取得进步 | You've made great progress. |
| make sense | 有意义 | It doesn't make sense. |
| make use of | 利用 | Make use of your time. |
| make sure | 确保 | Make sure you lock the door. |

### take 短语
| 短语 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| take action | 采取行动 | We must take action now. |
| take advantage of | 利用 | Take advantage of this opportunity. |
| take care of | 照顾 | Take care of yourself. |
| take part in | 参加 | I'll take part in the meeting. |
| take place | 发生 | The meeting took place yesterday. |
| take turns | 轮流 | Let's take turns to speak. |

### have 短语
| 短语 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| have access to | 有权使用 | I have access to the database. |
| have difficulty (in) doing | 做...有困难 | I have difficulty understanding. |
| have effect on | 对...有影响 | This has effect on health. |
| have nothing to do with | 与...无关 | It has nothing to do with me. |

## 学习建议

1. **整体记忆**：将搭配作为一个整体记忆，而非分开记单词
2. **语境学习**：通过例句理解搭配的使用场景
3. **对比学习**：注意近义搭配的区别（如 remind sb of vs remind sb to）
4. **主动使用**：在写作和口语中主动使用学过的搭配
